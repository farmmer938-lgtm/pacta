Pacta/Privacy

Privacy

What this site collects, and what it does not.

Summary

Pacta has no accounts, no advertising and no third-party trackers. The site sets no cookies.

Page analytics

Pages count anonymous, first-party aggregate page views through a small script on pacta.wtf (b.js): no cookies, no identifiers in browser storage, no IP address stored, and nothing is sent when your browser sends Do Not Track.

Live checks

When you press Check, the address and chain you entered are sent to Pacta's API at https://pacta-x402.pacta.workers.dev. "Verify live on RPC" sends read-only JSON-RPC calls from your browser directly to public RPC providers (publicnode.com, drpc.org, base.org), which see your IP address under their own policies.

Requests you send

The request form sends the track, protocols, scope text and, only if you enter one, a contact handle to Pacta's API so the team can answer. Do not include personal or sensitive information beyond a contact handle. GitHub issues and emails you choose to send are handled by those services.

Contact

Pacta by Fractal Neurons. Primary intake: pacta.wtf/request/. Contact: pacta@dri.network (the Fractal Neurons DRI desk, until pacta.wtf mail is live).
